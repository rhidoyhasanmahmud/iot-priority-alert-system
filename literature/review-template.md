# Critical paper review template

Copy only when reviewing a real paper. Keep claims specific, source-located and distinguishable from reviewer inference.

## 1. Bibliographic record and source

Stable ID; complete title/authors/year/venue/publisher; verified DOI or authoritative link; supplied version and page offset. State full-text/partial status and whether independently replicated. Record source fingerprint where available. Do not guess missing metadata.

## 2. Research problem

What decision is made, for whom, and which measurable objective matters? Define scope.

## 3. Authors' claimed contribution

State the precise methodological/theoretical/empirical claim. Separate what is proposed from what is demonstrated.

## 4. Model and assumptions

Resource, queues, service/preemption, traffic, capacity, metadata, future-information access, learning needs, replacement/loss semantics and metric definitions.

## 5. Method and decision mechanism

Inputs -> state -> admission/selection -> update. Give algorithm/equation locations. Explain why the rule should address the problem. Identify ambiguous reproduction choices.

## 6. Evidence ledger

| Claim examined | Page/section/table | Actual evidence | Interpretation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Record a real claim | Exact location | Result or theorem and its conditions | What it does not establish |

Include baseline fairness, tuning, repeats/uncertainty, negative outcomes and whether gains combine multiple interventions. Distinguish measurements from theoretical guarantees.

## 7. Critical appraisal

Assess internal, construct, statistical and external validity where applicable. Identify assumptions that materially change the conclusion. Distinguish author-stated limitations from your inference. Explain what evidence would resolve uncertainties; do not equate omission with absence.

## 8. Synthesis contribution and open question

What does this paper establish for the review? Which other papers agree or conflict? What question remains worth investigating? A limitation of one paper is not automatically a field-wide gap.

Optionally add one short Bangla reading note. Link to the comparison and reference register. Do not repeat the full English review in another file.
