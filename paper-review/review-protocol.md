# Literature review protocol

## Scope and selection

Study IoT/MQTT/edge alert scheduling, urgency, deadlines, burst/overload, aging, fairness and admission loss. Prioritize 2024-2026 research; include older foundational methods where necessary. This is a focused review, not an SLR unless a full systematic protocol is later adopted.

Search Google Scholar, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect and Springer Link. Record the actual search date, source and query when performed. Useful queries include `MQTT message prioritization`, `IoT deadline-aware scheduling`, `priority scheduling bursty traffic` and `IoT scheduling fairness overload`.

Include a paper when its decision mechanism or evaluation materially informs our research question. Exclude generic IoT applications/security papers with no relevant scheduling contribution. Do not select by citation count alone or claim an unverified quartile.

## Review workflow

1. Verify title, authors, year, venue, publisher and DOI from the paper and authoritative record.
2. Identify whether the supplied text is published, accepted manuscript or preprint; record page offsets.
3. Explain **What / Why / How / Evidence / So What**, English followed by Bangla details.
4. Trace claims to pages, algorithms, equations and tables. Separate author results, reviewer inference and teaching examples.
5. Record information/resource assumptions, loss accounting, baselines, tuning, uncertainty and contrary results.
6. Update the index and cross-paper matrix. Preserve IDs; do not duplicate a reviewed paper.
7. After 20-25 relevant reviews, compare candidate gaps and choose the idea before designing simulations.

## Completion criteria

A full-text review counts only when method and evaluation have been inspected. An abstract or metadata-only catalog entry does not count. Read the closest 5-8 methods deeply enough to explain their behavior, limitations and comparator requirements.

No final algorithm, external baseline, superiority claim or simulation matrix is selected during this collection phase. Maintain candidate ideas as hypotheses and examine contrary evidence. Award ambition must not influence exclusion of unfavorable findings.

## Lightweight collection record

For each additional paper, record title/DOI, discovery source/query/date, include/exclude reason, source version, review status and stable review ID. Do not fabricate historical search records.
